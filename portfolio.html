01 / Computer vision

Darts Computer-Vision Scoring Prototype

Detects dartboard keypoints, corrects perspective with homography, and maps a selected dart location to its standard score, using a YOLOv8-pose model trained on a ~2,000-image synthetic dataset and fine-tuned on real board images. It also includes full 501 game logic with double-out, bust, turn, and checkout handling.

Python · OpenCV · YOLOv8
